So, what is SignSpeak?

A prototype that helps people who use sign language (ASL) and people who don't understand each other, in real time. It turns signs into big text and voice, and typed or spoken words into signs.

🤟

Signer

You use sign language. Switch on the camera and sign. Your words show up in big text and can be spoken aloud for others.

💬

Speaker

You don't sign. Type, speak or tap a word. The app shows it as a big, slow sign that a signer can follow.
