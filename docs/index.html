SignSpeak

Help us make SignSpeak better

SignSpeak is a prototype that helps people who use sign language (ASL) and people who don't understand each other, in real time. It turns signs into big text and voice, and typed or spoken words into signs.

We want you to participate in a short survey regarding this app. Participation is voluntary and you can exit anytime.

We recommend using a laptop to try out the web application for the best experience.
